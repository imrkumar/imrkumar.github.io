Pairzy Privacy Policy

Publisher: Abhirah Tech
Contact: contactabhirahtech@gmail.com
Effective date: 20 September 2026
Application: Pairzy for Android (package com.abhirahtech.pairzy)

What stays on your device

Pairzy saves your theme, settings, scores, stars, gems, owned and equipped card styles, earned-reward receipts, daily challenge history, completed-game records and any unfinished round in a local database on this device. Names entered for same-device multiplayer stay inside that saved round. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no developer-run analytics or gameplay server. Android automatic backup is disabled for this app. Pairzy does not ask for your age or date of birth, and does not request location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera permissions.

Local game data is not sent to the publisher. Local game records remain until you reset local data, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to operating-system behavior.

Advertising and your consent choices

Pairzy uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to show an optional rewarded ad for an extra peek, and a limited ad when you continue from a finished journey level. Ads are shown to all users; the app does not ask for age. All ad traffic is tagged as child-directed, and Pairzy requests non-personalized ads with a G-rated creative cap. Non-personalized does not mean no personal data is processed.

Regional consent is checked before any ad request, and no ad is requested when your consent status does not allow it. Google may process IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location, device and account identifiers including the Android advertising identifier, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and similar data described in Google’s policies, for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Ads are never required to play: if an ad cannot load, your round continues.

See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites
  • AdMob and Google Play data disclosure

Ad placements

A player can opt in to an ad for an extra peek. Limited interstitial ads are only considered when continuing from a finished journey level. No app-open, banner, daily-mode or multiplayer ads are implemented. Gameplay remains usable offline when ads cannot connect.

What Pairzy does not do

It does not sell your data, run its own analytics or crash reporting, sync your progress to a server, take real-money payments, or share your saved game with anyone. Card styles are bought only with gems earned by playing.

Your choices and deletion

Reset my local data in Settings deletes your scores, gems, card styles, saved round and settings from this device. Google’s consent record is stored separately: reopen it with Settings → Privacy & ad choices → Manage consent choices, and use your Android system settings to manage advertising identifiers. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to your operating system’s behavior.

Requests to the publisher may be sent to contactabhirahtech@gmail.com. If you email support, your email provider and the publisher receive the content you send; do not include sensitive data unnecessarily. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Children

Pairzy does not collect age or date of birth, and ads are shown to all users with child-directed ad tagging as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child’s data has been collected through advertising, contact the publisher using the details above and the request will be handled.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text ships with the app and the date above changes. Nothing here grants the publisher rights it does not otherwise have.
