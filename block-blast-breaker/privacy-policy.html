Application: Block Blast Breaker for Android and iOS (package com.blockblastbreaker.app)

What stays on your device

Block Blast Breaker saves your levels, stars, coins, cosmetics, statistics, settings and any unfinished game in local storage on this device. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no developer-run analytics or gameplay server — none of this data is transmitted to us. Crash diagnostics are written to a local, on-device log capped at 25 entries; that log contains only game state (such as the current level and score), is never uploaded automatically, and can be shared manually from the crash screen through your device’s own share sheet. The app does not request your age or date of birth, and does not request microphone, camera, contacts, precise location or storage permissions.

Local game data is not sent to the publisher. Local game records remain until you reset progress, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to operating-system behavior.

Advertising and your consent choices

Block Blast Breaker uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to show rewarded videos you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. Even though our own code collects no personal data, the embedded Google advertising SDK collects data to serve, cap and measure ads. This includes the Android advertising identifier (or, on iOS, the identifier for advertisers when you permit tracking), device and app information such as device model, operating system and app version, language and approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data such as impressions, completions and clicks. The app requests non-personalized ads with a maximum ad content rating of “G” so served ads stay suitable for a general audience. Non-personalized does not mean no personal data is processed.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region with equivalent rules, the app presents Google’s certified consent form (UMP) the first time it starts, before any ad request, and no ad is requested until you have made a choice. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad Privacy Settings, which reopens the same consent form — withdrawal is as easy as granting. You can also limit ad personalization at the operating-system level: on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads, to delete the advertising identifier or opt out of ads personalization; on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and disable “Allow Apps to Request to Track”. On iOS the app asks for tracking permission through Apple’s standard App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising SDK initializes; declining does not restrict any game feature, you simply receive less relevant ads. If you are a California resident, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA; Google’s handling of advertising data is described in the Google policies linked below.

Ad placements

Rewarded video is the only ad format in the game, and each placement is one you opt into: an extra revive to continue a run, double coins at the end of a game, a gameplay boost, and a double daily reward. No banner, interstitial, app-open or other ads are implemented. Ads are never required to play: if an ad cannot load, every game feature remains fully usable.

What Block Blast Breaker does not do

It does not sell your data, run its own analytics or crash reporting, sync your progress to a server, take real-money payments, or share your saved game with anyone. Coins, cosmetics and boosts are earned by playing and spent inside the game only.

Permissions

Notifications (optional) are used only to remind you about daily rewards and streaks. The permission is requested only when you turn reminders on in Settings, never at launch, and no notification data leaves your device. Modify audio settings is used to play game sound effects and music, and vibration provides haptic feedback during gameplay.

Your choices and deletion

Change or withdraw ad consent in Settings → Privacy → Ad Privacy Settings (shown where a consent form applies). Disable reminders in Settings → Daily Reminders. Deleting your scores, coins, cosmetics, statistics and settings from this device is Settings → Reset Progress; uninstalling the app removes all local data, including the crash log, subject to your operating system’s behavior.

Requests to the publisher may be sent to contactabhirahtech@gmail.com. If you email support, your email provider and the publisher receive the content you send; do not include sensitive data unnecessarily. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Children

This app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, it is not enrolled in the Google Play Families program or Apple’s Kids Category, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. It contains no user-generated content, no chat and no social features. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child’s data has been collected through advertising, contact the publisher using the details above and the request will be handled.
