Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · Version 1 (app v0.4.0) · Abhirah Tech
Short version: Packonaut stores your game progress only on your device. We have no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. The game shows ads — rewarded videos you choose to watch, and occasional ads shown between levels (never during a puzzle, and capped at a few per day) — and Google's ad system processes some device data as described below. You can erase everything by resetting progress in Settings or uninstalling.

1. Who we are

Packonaut ("the game") is published by Abhirah Tech ("we", abhirahtech.com). Contact for any privacy question: [email protected].

2. Data stored on your device

  • Game progress, stars, lamps, daily streak, settings and preferences are saved in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you delete the app.
  • Reset progress in Settings deletes this data instantly.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC): rewarded videos (always your choice) and occasional interstitial ads between puzzles. Interstitials are frequency-capped (a few per day at most) and never interrupt a puzzle in progress.

Before ads can be shown in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (UMP) lets you choose whether ads may be personalized — you can change that choice any time via Settings → Privacy options.

AdMob, as an independent controller, processes device data such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID) to select and measure ads
  • Approximate location (IP-based country/city level) and device characteristics to comply with ad content rules and frequency caps
  • App interaction data (e.g. ad views/completions) for reward delivery and fraud prevention

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Where required, consent is requested via the in-game form, and you can withdraw it at any time.

We never receive your name, email, contacts or location. We cannot identify you personally.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, does not knowingly collect their data, and ad content is requested with a maximum content rating of PG. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

5. Permissions

The game requests no permissions beyond what Android requires to run. It does not access your camera, microphone (explicitly blocked in the build), location, files or contacts.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data on servers, the fastest path is: Settings → Reset progress (erases game data on-device) and/or uninstall the app; Google offers ad-related controls via your Google account's Ad Settings.

7. Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change (for example, if optional purchases are introduced in a future version). The version and date above always reflect the current policy.
