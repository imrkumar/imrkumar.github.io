This is the privacy policy for Untangle!, a game by Abhirah Tech.

What the game stores

Your progress, coins, stars and preferences are saved on this device only — a local database on Android, browser storage on the web. There is no game account and no game server.

Deleting your progress in Settings removes that local profile. Uninstalling the app removes its local database.

What the game does not do

No account, no sign-in, no cloud save.

No third-party analytics SDK, no advertising identifier of our own, no contact list, no photos, no microphone, no location.

Gameplay works fully offline.

Advertising

Rewarded video is optional and only ever grants the reward you were shown. The browser companion and the default Android build request no ads at all.

After clearing a board, an interstitial ad may occasionally appear before the next one — paced so it never interrupts play: never before the eighth campaign clear, at most every fourth clear or depth, capped per session, and never on a failed board or while a board is open. Zen depths clear less often than other modes.

Android builds with advertising enabled use Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure those ads. Where required by law, a consent request is shown before any ad loads, and the ad build follows Google’s own data-safety disclosures.

Test-ad builds use Google’s official sample ad units; they are internal and never published.

Purchases

Coins and boosters are earned by playing or granted by watching an optional ad. There are no in-app purchases in this build and no way to spend real money.

Children

The game is a puzzle with no violence, no chat and no user-generated content, and it is not directed at children. The store listing carries an honest content rating, including the presence of optional rewarded ads.

Your choices

Sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, colour-blind palette and relaxed mode are all in Settings. Advertising privacy choices are available in the app wherever ads are enabled.

Questions, or a request about your data: use the developer contact details on our Google Play listing (Untangle! Arrow Puzzle) or open an issue at https://github.com/imrkumar/untangle.
