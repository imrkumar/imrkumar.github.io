1 · Scope

App and publisher

This policy applies to Numbora, a number-pairing puzzle published by Abhirah Tech. It covers the Android app (com.abhirahtech.numbora) and the Numbora web version. The web version does not include an advertising SDK; Android ad behavior depends on the build you install.

When this policy says “we,” it means Abhirah Tech. We do not require registration, create player accounts, or operate a server that receives your puzzle progress.

2 · On-device data

Game data stored on your device

Numbora saves the information needed to resume play in the app’s private on-device storage. This can include active boards, completed levels, scores, streaks, builder presets, and your settings. The save is not sent to Abhirah Tech, and the game does not use a Numbora account or a Numbora analytics service.

Your progress is stored until you reset it, clear Numbora’s app storage, or uninstall the app. A web browser may apply its own storage and deletion controls to the web version.

3 · Advertising

Ads and Google Mobile Ads

Ad behavior depends on the Android build. Numbora’s web version has no ads. The closed-testing Android build is configured with Google demo rewarded-ad units. A production build can be ad-free; a separately configured ad-enabled build can use live Google AdMob units.

When an ad-enabled Android build is installed

The only ad placement in Numbora is an optional rewarded video: you choose Watch ad when asking for an extra hint. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized and a rewarded ad may be preloaded when the app starts, so an ad request and related data processing can happen before you tap that button. Numbora does not show banner, interstitial, or app-open ads.

Demo ads still involve the ad SDK. Google’s demo ad units do not earn advertising revenue for the publisher, but using them is not the same as having no SDK or no network requests. The SDK may communicate with Google while initializing or loading a test ad.

Depending on the build, device, region, and consent state, Google Mobile Ads may collect or receive information such as your IP address (which can indicate an approximate region), advertising or app/device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention data. Google says the data types in its current SDK disclosure are encrypted in transit using TLS. Google may use information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and related purposes under its own policies. We do not control Google’s independent processing.

Live-ad builds use Google’s consent tools where required before live ad requests and provide an in-app ad privacy-options entry when that flow is enabled. The current closed-test demo configuration does not show the live-mode consent form. Before changing the ad configuration, we will update this policy and the Play Console disclosures to match the released build.

Learn more in Google’s How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4 · Sharing

Sharing and third parties

Abhirah Tech does not sell your game save or share it with other companies. We do not run a Numbora account service, analytics backend, social feed, or player-to-player messaging.

In ad-enabled Android builds, Google processes ad-related information as described above. If you choose to open an external link or email support, the destination provider receives the information needed to handle that action. Their own privacy policies apply.

5 · Device access

Permissions

The Android app needs internet access for ad-enabled builds and external links, and vibration access for optional haptic feedback. The ad SDK may add its own network or advertising-identifier permissions. Numbora does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, shared files, or precise location.

6 · Your choices

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete local progress: open Settings and choose Reset all local progress, or clear the app’s storage/uninstall it. This removes the save held on that device.
  • Choose whether to watch: rewarded ads are optional. You can dismiss the offer; the game does not require watching an ad to keep playing.
  • Manage ad privacy: where live ads and Google’s consent flow are enabled, use Ad privacy choices in About. You can also review Android’s advertising and privacy settings. Available controls vary by device and region.
  • Ask us a question: email contactabhirahtech@gmail.com. We cannot delete information held independently by Google; use Google’s privacy controls for requests about Google-held data.
7 · Children

Children’s privacy

Numbora is a general-audience number puzzle and is not designed or directed to children under 13. We do not ask for a player’s age or knowingly collect personal information directly from children. In ad-enabled builds, Google’s SDK may process device and ad information as described above; the Play target-audience and ad configuration must be reviewed before release and whenever they change.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email, contact us and we will review the request. For information processed by Google, please also use Google’s privacy controls.

8 · Updates

Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when we do. If a change affects data handling, we will provide any additional notice required by law or Google Play.

Questions about Numbora’s privacy practices can be sent to contactabhirahtech@gmail.com. Publisher: Abhirah Tech.
